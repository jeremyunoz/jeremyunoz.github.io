These assets identify the technologies shown in the portfolio. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners; their inclusion does not imply endorsement.

- `c.svg`, `cplusplus.svg`, `raspberrypi.svg`, `arduino.svg`, `python.svg`, `pytorch.svg`, `react.svg`, `git.svg`, `github.svg`, and `linux.svg`: [Devicon v2.16.0](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/v2.16.0/icons), distributed under the [MIT license](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/blob/v2.16.0/LICENSE). Individual logos remain subject to their owners' trademark policies.
- `espressif.png`: [Espressif's official primary logo](https://www.espressif.com/sites/all/themes/espressif/images/logo-guidelines/primary-logo.png). See [Espressif's logo guidelines](https://www.espressif.com/en/about-espressif/logo-guidelines), including minimum size and clear space. All Espressif's logos are trademarks of Espressif Systems (Shanghai) Co., Ltd.
- `freertos.png`: [FreeRTOS official site](https://github.freertos.org/media/2023/logo.png). FreeRTOS is a trademark of Amazon Web Services, Inc. or its affiliates.
- `mqtt.svg`: [Simple Icons MQTT](https://simpleicons.org/?q=mqtt), retrieved through `https://cdn.simpleicons.org/mqtt/660066`; the icon collection is [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md), subject to the mark owner's trademark rights.
- `ultralytics.svg`: [Ultralytics official asset repository](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ultralytics/assets/main/logo/Ultralytics_Logotype_Original.svg). See the [Ultralytics brand guidelines](https://www.ultralytics.com/brand).
- `awsiot.svg`, `dynamodb.svg`: AWS architecture service icons mirrored by [AWS Fundamentals](https://awsfundamentals.com/aws-icons), for AWS IoT Core and Amazon DynamoDB respectively. Refer to the [AWS Architecture Icons usage guidelines](https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/icons/) for usage conditions.

The `-transparent` AWS variants remove the background and use its color for the original icon. The transparent FreeRTOS variants remove the white fill and use a light wordmark on dark backgrounds. `espressif-dark.svg` retains the official mark and uses the white wordmark shown in Espressif's dark-background guidance.
- `ultralytics-dark.svg`: [Ultralytics official reverse logotype](https://github.com/ultralytics/assets/blob/main/logo/Ultralytics_Logotype_Reverse.svg).
- `github-dark.svg`: [Simple Icons GitHub](https://simpleicons.org/?q=github), in white for dark surfaces.
- `espressif-official.svg`: [Espressif's official documentation logo](https://github.com/espressif/esp-idf/blob/master/docs/_static/espressif-logo.svg); it has a gray wordmark.
- `freertos-clean.svg`: [FreeRTOS 2005 logo on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:FreeRTOS_logo_2005.svg); this historical vector includes a white area as part of the logo artwork.
